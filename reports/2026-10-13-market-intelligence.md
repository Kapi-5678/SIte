## 🚀 Space & Satellite Sector (Rocket Lab, BlackSky, Redwire, AST SpaceMobile)

* **Rocket Lab USA (RKLB)**
  - **Past 24H News:** Shares fell 3.7% to ~$65.71 on October 12, extending a pullback from the 52-week high of $151.00. The company recently signed its **largest commercial Electron contract to date** — a multi-year deal to fly 20 Synspective missions, bringing contracted Synspective launches to 47 and total launch backlog above 100 missions. Iridium shareholders approved the planned Rocket Lab acquisition (targeting close by mid-2027), though the company abandoned a $3.6B loan financing in favor of stock sales to fund the deal. TD Cowen lowered its target to $90 from $120; Barclays initiated with an Overweight.
  - **Upcoming Events/Catalysts:** **Neutron first launch targeted Q4 2026** (maiden flight delayed after a stage-1 tank test failure). Iridium deal close expected by **mid-2027**. Watch for Q3 2026 earnings (estimated November 2026).

* **BlackSky Technology (BKSY)**
  - **Past 24H News:** Shares fell 5.1% to ~$19.86 on October 12. On **October 8**, the company won a **six-figure Gen-3 On-Demand pilot contract with a new international intelligence customer**, expanding its sovereign partner base. Craig Hallum held a virtual management meeting October 8.
  - **Upcoming Events/Catalysts:** Q3 2026 earnings expected **November 2026** (Q2 results in August showed 50% YoY revenue growth to $33.3M). Gen-3 satellite launch schedule and further sovereign contract wins are key catalysts to watch.

* **Redwire Corporation (RDW)**
  - **Past 24H News:** Shares fell 3.6% to ~$9.24 on October 12 amid broader space sector weakness. On **October 12**, Redwire confirmed the **final pair of ROSA solar array wings will launch to the ISS aboard SpaceX CRS-35**. Other recent developments include: (1) a follow-on contract for Axiom Space Station Module 2 solar arrays (Oct 5); (2) a Honda robotics partnership for commercial space stations (Oct 1); (3) **CFO retirement with succession plan announced** October 7; (4) **Bain Capital filed to sell ~10M shares**, pressuring the stock. Edge Autonomy is reshaping Redwire's revenue mix toward defense. Analyst consensus is "Hold" with $14.69 average price target.
  - **Upcoming Events/Catalysts:** **SpaceX CRS-35 launch** (imminent — carries the final ROSA wings). Q3 2026 earnings expected **November 2026**. Bain Capital secondary offering execution is a near-term overhang.

* **AST SpaceMobile (ASTS)** ⚠️ *— Highest priority today*
  - **Past 24H News:** **ASTS fell ~15% intraday on October 12** following news that SpaceX purchased low-band spectrum for ~$8B, raising competitive concerns for direct-to-device satellite coverage. Shares partially recovered and were trading near ~$53, still down ~27% YTD from a 52-week range of $47.57–$133.86. **Deutsche Bank upgraded to Buy** with a $93 target, calling the selloff a buying opportunity. **B. Riley cut to Neutral** from Buy, slashing target to $65 from $85, citing competition, delayed launches, and high costs. Separately, the **next batch of BlueBird satellites (BBS 8, 9, 10) arrived at Cape Canaveral** ahead of launch. A TELUS integration test succeeded with standard phones connecting directly to AST satellites; Canadian commercial launch targeted within ~12 months. Law firms are soliciting investors for a securities class action (lead plaintiff deadline **November 13, 2026**).
  - **Upcoming Events/Catalysts:** **BlueBird 8/9/10 launch imminent from Cape Canaveral** (no confirmed date yet; company targets ~45 satellites in orbit by year-end 2026, aiming to hit milestone by November). Q3 2026 earnings expected **November 2026**.
